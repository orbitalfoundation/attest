## Relevance to attest

- **The same instinct for sign-in, in a different stack.** A passkey rooted to a DID, no passwords,
  no long-lived bearer tokens. OpenVTC puts the passkey's public key into the DID document; attest
  publishes passkey keys at `/by/<did>` and has the passkey sign delegations. Putting attest
  passkeys into the did:plc document as an extra verification method is worth considering.
- **OpenID is inside it, as a specification.** SIOPv2 is an OpenID specification; OpenVTC uses it
  for site sign-in. Nothing read says the OpenID Foundation itself is part of this group.
- **Opposite choices on visibility and personhood.** OpenVTC: private, pairwise relationships,
  vetted personhood with identity documents. attest: public signed statements, no personhood
  check, trust from a root you choose. Its credential "correlation scope" (pairwise, directed,
  public) is a precise vocabulary for a choice attest makes implicitly (everything public, except
  where you sign in).
- **Agents.** The slides' "agent names" (`domain/@name`) and verifiable delegation credentials;
  here, the Verifiable Trust Agent is the person's own agent holding their keys.
