# Prior art for the attestation and emitter-scoring service

Provenance: compiled 2026-09-22 by a Claude Code session (Fable 5.1) for
`orbital/attest`, from pages fetched that day with WebFetch, curl and
pdftotext. Every row carries the URL actually read and the access date.
Where a page did not state something the cell says "not stated". Nothing
in this file comes from memory; where a fact was not on the page read it
is marked unknown or the source is listed under "Could not access".
Quotations are verbatim from the page cited. Star and commit counts are
what GitHub showed on the day and are only rough liveness signals.

What we are building, for reference while reading: ordinary sign-up with
a hosted keypair behind it; signed attestations about URLs and emitters
(upvote, comment, "I know this author", supports/challenges a claim); a
public read endpoint any static site can query for scores; later, trust
by flow from a root through vouches so sybil clusters are bounded, plus
track record from claims that resolved. Identity is not soul-bound: an
emitter can burn an identity and re-earn trust on a new one.

Column key: "Separable" = can the attestation/score layer live apart from
where the content is hosted.

## What is closest to what we want

1. AT Protocol labels plus Ozone: signed, revocable, DID-sourced annotations about content, distributed as a firehose separate from hosting, with an open moderation UI. Gap: targets are at:// and did: only, and labels are tags with no score, no vouching and no track record.
2. Advogato trust metric and Levien's metadata HOWTO: flow from a seed through peer certifications with a per-level capacity bound, generalised to "assertions" about arbitrary identifiers. Gap: dead since 2016, one seed, no notion of resolved claims or of burning an identity.
3. Ethereum Attestation Service off-chain mode: schema, attester, recipient, refUID, revocable, expiry, signed and verifiable off-chain. Gap: wallet identity and chain gravity; no scoring or trust computation at all.
4. X Community Notes with the QSMF per-rater weight: bridging consensus across a latent disagreement axis, with rater quality learned jointly. Gap: needs tens of millions of ratings, scores only X posts, and the contributor identity is an X account.
5. Nostr kind 17 reactions with NIP-73 targets and the nostr-wot oracle: today's only running system where anyone signs a reaction to an arbitrary URL and a separate service answers "how far is this key from me". Gap: distance is not trust (their words), no root, no bound on sybil clusters, no track record.

Nothing found combines all four of: ordinary sign-up with a hosted key, signed attestations on arbitrary URLs and emitters, a public read endpoint for static sites, and trust by bounded flow plus resolved-claim track record with burnable identity. The burnable-identity rule has a named cost in the literature (whitewashing; Seradji & Fallah 2017, Hoffman et al.) and the Friedman & Resnick paper must be read in full before it is designed.
