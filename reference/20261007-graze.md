## Relevance to attest

The top layer of attest's stack, already working at scale on AT Protocol: tools that decide what
a person reads. A trust signal from attest ("posts from people trusted by people I trust") would
be a natural feed input once scoring exists; the route in is either a user list attest keeps up to
date (if a Graze node can follow one) or a node that asks an outside ranking service, which would
need Graze's team. The "Verified News" feed and its operator are a natural reader for The Future
Times. Tension: an advertising market, where attest has no metrics to sell; their stated
transparency is the common ground.
