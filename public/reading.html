reading room what the people here have read

Papers, specifications and projects on trust, identity and communities, each filed by someone who read it, with their note. It is built from ordinary attest bookmarks: anyone can add to it by saving a page with the tag reading-room, from the bookmarklet or the save window, alongside tags for its topic. Items filed by more people rise.

For the history of this field in order, see history. For a far larger, human-verified catalogue of decentralized tools with capture-risk ratings, see OpenHaven. Our own working catalogue, with the address and date each source was read, is in the repository.
