How it works

1Sign in with your phoneYour face or fingerprint unlock is your key. No password, no email, nothing to remember.
2Save and vouch as you readTag any page from any site, with a button in your browser or your phone's share menu. Say which ones you stand behind.
3See through trusted eyesSee what the people you trust saved, vouched for and said, on their own pages and under each tag.

Why

Much of what reaches us is pushed by people we would never trust if we could see who they were. We end up judging each post by its content, squinting at a photograph to decide whether it is real. attest lets you look at the envelope instead: who sent this, who stands behind them, and how that has turned out before. It is built for people who want to find each other, and act together, on the things that matter, including the living world we share.

The internet is changing shape. Trust is moving out of the platforms in the middle and into systems with no center, where people vouch for each other directly and anyone can check the record. We are changing too. More of what each of us does online will be done by agents, so "you" becomes your own will plus the powers you hand to them, and can take back. attest keeps that legible: your agents sign with their own keys, under limits your passkey signed, and anyone can see who answers for what they say.
