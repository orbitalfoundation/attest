save from anywhere bookmarklet and phones

Save any page you are reading, with tags and a note, and vouch for it if you want to. A small attest window opens with the page's address and title; you sign there, with attest's own session, so the page you are reading never touches your key.

On a computer

Drag this to your bookmarks bar, then click it on any page:

＋ attest

On an Android phone

  1. Open attest.monster in Chrome and sign in.
  2. Menu ⋮ → Add to home screen (or Install app).
  3. From then on, Share → attest appears for any link, in any app.

On an iPhone or iPad

Safari does not let websites join the share sheet, so use the bookmarklet:

  1. Copy this:
  2. In Safari, bookmark any page (share button → Add Bookmark), naming it "＋ attest".
  3. Open bookmarks, Edit, tap the new one, and replace its address with what you copied.
  4. On any page, tap the address bar, choose "＋ attest" from your bookmarks.

If you prefer the share sheet on iPhone, a Shortcut can do it: make one that receives URLs from the share sheet and opens https://attest.monster/save?url= followed by the shared URL.

Where saves go

Each bookmark is a monster.attest.bookmark record in your own AT Protocol repository, signed by your key: the address, title, tags and note. Filing a page under a tag is not the same as vouching for it; the checkbox adds a separate upvote. Your bookmarks are on your page at attest.monster/yourname, and everything filed under a word is at attest.monster/tag/word.
