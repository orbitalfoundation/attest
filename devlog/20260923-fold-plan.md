# 2026-09-23 — decision: fold into AT Protocol; the sprint

Point-in-time. Anselm: "It seems fine to me if we have an inline
signature. I like using passkeys… I feel like we have to fold in. We
may have to sprint to a more compatible more complete feature set
before any adoption." Decision taken. This entry is the plan.

## Invariants (his)

- Passkey sign-up and sign-in, no password the person ever sees.
- Every record carries the person's own inline signature (device key
  delegated from the passkey), verifiable with the key alone.
- Ordinary sites and his other projects get sign-in and attestations
  from one service with one script tag.
- Public by construction; root-as-parameter scoring; burnable identity.

## Shape after the fold

```
passkey ──delegates──▶ device key ──signs inline──▶ record ──▶ user's atproto repo (our PDS, or a linked one)
                                                        │
                                   relay / firehose ────┴──▶ attest index (AppView): /read /by /site, scores from a root
```

- **Identity**: `did:plc` account on our PDS, created at passkey
  sign-up with a server-side secret the person never sees; handle
  `<name>.attest.monster`. A person with a Bluesky handle links it via
  the existing `bsky:` proof of control plus atproto OAuth for write
  access; records then go to their own repo.
- **Records**: `app.certified.*` shapes where they fit (follow, badge
  award, evaluation, signature proof), one lexicon of ours,
  `monster.attest.*`, for URL, DOI and hash subjects (vote, comment,
  claim, statement), each with badge.blue-style `signatures` carrying
  the device key's inline signature.
- **Index**: attest subscribes to its PDS's repo stream (and later the
  relay firehose) and rebuilds what `/read`, `/by`, `/site` serve.
  The SQLite log becomes derived state; `/log` becomes a pointer to
  the repos.
- **Scoring**: unchanged design, now over records from repos, plus
  read-in of `sh.tangled.graph.vouch`, `sh.tangled.graph.follow` and
  `app.bsky.graph.follow` as weighted edge types.
- **Widget and package**: same API; `configure({server})` unchanged.

## Anselm's worry: "exe.dev may not be the industrial scalable fabric
we want… they could shut down some day"

True of any host. What the fold buys is that the host is replaceable:
identities are `did:plc` in a public directory, and the PLC rotation
key that controls every account made here is backed up off the box;
repo data lives in `/srv/pds`, pulled nightly to canada1
(`~/backups/attest/pull.sh`, cron 03:17); the index is rebuildable
from the repos. Moving means restoring `/srv/pds` on any Linux box,
putting the secrets back, and pointing DNS. Records people wrote also
propagate to relays and mirrors outside our control. A dedicated
server is easy later and unnecessary for the spike.

## Phase 2 landed, 2026-09-24 early morning

- Sign-up creates the repo account on our PDS (`<handle>.attest.monster`,
  `did:plc`), password server-side; the account's did is the `did:plc`,
  delegations root there; the passkey's `did:key` is kept as `key_did`.
- Client builds lexicon records and signs inline (`inlineSign`);
  server verifies against the delegated device key and the repo did,
  validates against the lexicon, writes with deterministic rkeys for
  vote/vouch/claim (base32 sha256 of the subject) or tids otherwise,
  indexes the result. Retract = signed delete. Service verifications
  go into the service's own repo (`attest.attest.monster`).
- 33 + 10 checks pass locally against the live PDS and 33 against the
  live service. A deploy briefly broke the site: `@atproto/lexicon`
  was a dev dependency; moved.
- Open: wildcard certificate for handle hostnames not yet issued by
  exe.dev after ~1 h (plan B: Cloudflare proxy for `*.attest.monster`
  with a transform rule preserving the host); firehose consumer
  (phase 3); external account linking (phase 4); technical page and
  llms.txt still describe the log in places (accuracy pass).

## Phase 3 landed, 2026-09-24 morning

- `server/firehose.mjs` follows the PDS's `subscribeRepos` with
  `@atproto/sync` (unauthenticated commits, since it is our own PDS),
  filtered to our collections, cursor in a `meta` table. Create and
  update → verify the inline signature against the repo did and index;
  delete → retract; account events → status; identity events → repo
  handle. Proven with a record written straight into a throwaway repo
  by a separate client: indexed; an unsigned one ignored; delete
  retracted it. Both suites still pass with the consumer on.
- Handles bind by DNS (`server/dns.mjs`): `_atproto.<handle>` TXT
  through the Cloudflare zone API at account creation. Bluesky's
  public resolver answers for `attest.attest.monster` within minutes.
  The exe.dev wildcard certificate remains unissued after two hours;
  it is now only needed for the handle hostnames' HTTPS well-known,
  which DNS makes optional.
- Deleting an account at the PDS still leaves its index rows; account
  events from the firehose now record status, and hiding inactive
  accounts' records from counts is the next small step.

## 2026-09-26: three next steps

- **Inactive accounts hidden** from every count, site page and vouch
  list (`status` from firehose account events). e2e: a deleted
  account's comment drops 1 → 0; status recorded as `deleted`.
- **Graph read-in** (`server/graph.mjs`). Confirmed from real records:
  `sh.tangled.graph.vouch` names its subject in the rkey (a DID), with
  `kind` vouch|denounce and optional `reason`; Tangled and Bluesky
  follows use `subject`. On a verified `bsky:` proof we resolve the
  handle, link it (`identity_links`), list the three collections from
  its PDS and store `edges` (kinds `tangled.vouch`, `tangled.denounce`,
  `tangled.follow`, `bsky.follow`), refreshed every 6 h. Provisional
  weights in `graph.WEIGHTS` for the scorer. Profiles show edges
  between members. Limit: inbound edges from non-members need a
  backlink index (Constellation), not built.
- **Lexicons published**: `com.atproto.lexicon.schema` records (rkey =
  NSID) in the service repo, TXT `_lexicon.attest.monster` → its DID.
