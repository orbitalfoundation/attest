## The problem

Nothing on the web scores the people and outlets that feed us, and
every site that wants votes or comments has to build identity from
scratch or hand its content to a platform.

Anselm: "I liken this to the food and drug administration, or to a
river; I am being fed pollutants and there isn't even any scoring."
PageRank was an emitter score; the platforms removed it and score
items, or nothing. From the builder's side, a site like The Future
Times should not have to run its own votes, comments, sign-up and
password reset; handing those to a platform means the platform owns
the moat. The two problems are one service: a public bag of signed
statements about URLs and about keys, plus scoring computed over it.
"Anybody can build the rest of the fabric themselves."

## Goals

Two goals, deliberately separate: infrastructure any site can use, and
one opinion computed over it.

1. Micro-services that do not need their own authentication. One
   sign-up, a hosted keypair; any site accepts signed attestations
   from that key without running accounts of its own.
2. Subjective social-trust-graph scoring. Given a root and the public
   bag of vouches, how much is any emitter trusted as seen from that
   root. "Trusted by people you trust", never "trustworthy".
3. Track record: claims with resolution dates, scored on calibration.
4. Public analytics without a moat: votes, comments, vouches and reach
   for a site's URLs, public to competitors too.
5. Burnable identity: reputation belongs to the key, not the person.

## Principles fixed so far

- Score emitters, not items.
- The key is the identity; person, site, byline are proofs about it.
- Identity is disposable, so newcomers start at zero (Friedman and
  Resnick 2001, cited, not yet read).
- Sybils bounded by construction: flow from a root; local question only.
- Vouches cost the voucher: finite out-capacity, age-weighted edges, a
  vouch is a claim that can resolve false.
- Negatives do not propagate; an emitter is pulled down only by its own
  resolved record.
- Slow trust: standing rises at a bounded rate.
- Attestations are the commons; scoring is a client.
- Public by construction, reads private.
- Reuse before invent (AT Protocol first).
- No party colours, by arithmetic not policy.

## What the service must provide

| Piece | What | Shape to reuse |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Ordinary sign-up minting a hosted keypair; export and burn; optional proofs | atproto accounts; did:web for sites; SIWE message layout |
| Attestation record | One signed statement from a key about a URL, DOI or key; kinds: upvote, comment, vouch, claim, supports, challenges, resolution, retraction | atproto label envelope plus score field and new kinds; EAS chaining fields; NIP-73 target grammar |
| Log | Append-only, mirrorable, auditable | CT shape, not a chain; a PDS repo may already give this (verify) |
| Read endpoint | Root plus batch of targets; trust-weighted results naming the root; reads unlinkable; rate limits and caching | labeler queryLabels and subscribeLabels |
| Scoring | Flow with capacity bound, age weighting, bounded rise; Brier for claims | Advogato, EigenTrust, SybilRank, QSMF |
| Clients | Desk render; browser plugin; retrieval-agent batch client; institutional keys vouching bylines | Community Notes bridging; Polymarket optimistic window |

Two-tier attestation: institutions sign content and attest bylines;
the author's key is optional; pseudonymous key plus institutional
voucher is first-class for sources under threat. The first mass
consumer may be a model, not a person.

## Existing work

Nothing found combines hosted-key sign-up, signed attestations on
arbitrary URLs and emitters, a static-site read endpoint, and bounded
flow trust plus track record with burnable identity. Nearest: atproto
labels and Ozone; Advogato and Levien's HOWTO; EAS off-chain;
Community Notes with QSMF; Nostr kind 17 with NIP-73 and nostr-wot.
Theory: EigenTrust, SybilGuard, SybilRank, TrustRank. Shapes to reuse
from VCs, SIWE, Metaculus, GJO, Manifold, Polymarket, Wikipedia
perennial sources, Human Passport, PoH, BrightID, Farcaster, giscus,
Remark42, Hypothesis, Webmention. Avoid: staff-rated outlet raters
with a bias axis, GDI, Disqus lock-in, the dead ones. Named in a
pasted overview and not yet catalogued: NIP-32, Verax, C2PA,
Certificate Transparency, OpenTimestamps, Keybase, Scuttlebutt. The
full table is `reference/20260922-prior-art.md`.

## Open questions

Who resolves a claim; PDS or standalone; log shape; how reads stay
private; vouch expiry; did:web or did:plc for our accounts; sign-in
providers; hosting; the name; read Friedman and Resnick in full.

Step 2 is the smallest thing that satisfies goal 1; step 5 the smallest
that satisfies goal 2.
